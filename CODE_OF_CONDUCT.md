# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the IntelliStock community a respectful and
welcoming experience for everyone. We are committed to fostering an open,
inclusive, and friendly environment regardless of a contributor's background,
experience level, identity, or beliefs.

## Our standards

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its
official Code of Conduct. The full text is available at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short, we expect everyone in this community to:

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate in all interactions.
- Welcome newcomers and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to make things right.
- Prioritize what is best for the community over personal preferences.

Unacceptable behaviour includes (but is not limited to) personal attacks,
discriminatory language or jokes, sustained disruption of discussions,
publishing others' private information without consent, and any other
conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the
GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, the Discord
server (if applicable), and any other forum where someone is
representing the IntelliStock project or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of
Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer:

**krishnatechpranav@gmail.com**

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and safety of the
reporter.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the process described in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct and may take any action they deem appropriate in response to
behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
Actions may include a private warning, a public warning, a temporary
ban, or a permanent ban from the community, following the
**Contributor Covenant Enforcement Guidelines** at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines
