INTELLISTOCK - THE OBJECTIVE

Grow the account 1x-3x a year (+100% to +300%), beating SPY in every regime,
eventually on real money. Two-month equivalents: 1x = +12%, 2x = +20%, 3x = +25%.

The mechanism is NOT grinding small edges across many positions. It is catching
the big movers and holding them at size. A +100% name at a 2% position is noise;
at 10-15% it is the year.

Bull/chop - buy names making large sustained moves, enter while the move still
has room, size so one winner matters, hold.
Bear - do not sit in cash. Rotate into SQQQ (-3x inverse QQQ) and profit from the
downtrend. Trim the long book, hold while the downtrend persists, exit on a real
turn not noise.
Recovery - get back in fast; reversals start the largest moves.


THE GAP IS CONVERSION, NOT DISCOVERY

Discovery already finds the winners. One two-month window surfaced AAOI +102%,
VICR +80%, VIAV +66%, SNDK +65%, LASR +54%, TTMI +52%, AMAT +32%, ADI +28% -
none were bought. SNDK alone emitted 13 buy signals, all refused by portfolio
construction.

Four names at ~10% of NAV each, capturing half of a 60% move, is ~+12% per
two-month window, which is ~+100%/yr. The raw material is already there.

Do not hunt more names. Ask why a name the system ranked #1 did not get bought.


WHAT BLOCKS IT

1. Entry timing - winners bought late. One entered at 96.7% of the way through
   its move; another 19 days after the gap that was its whole +54.7%, with a
   measured maximum of +0.87% ever available.
2. Sizing - per-name satellite weight is mean 6.75% / median 4.73% of NAV.
3. No trim-back - the satellite cap is entry-only, so a great name is refused
   because a mediocre one sits on the budget (test_A11, fails on purpose).
4. Execution cost - every fill crosses the spread. Passive execution is the
   documented "largest unexploited cost lever" (simulated_execution.py:135).
5. Bear leg unvalidated - built, but not yet shown to profit in a bear.

CONSTRAINTS

- Turnover is the known leak: ~290%/mo live vs ~50%/mo break-even (23-35% for
  this specific edge). Prefer fewer, larger, longer-held positions.
- doc-179 / alpaca-main is real money, currently stopped. Nothing ships there
  without explicit sign-off.
- Never push to main while a backtest is running - it auto-deploys, the restart
  kills the run, and the run is unrecoverable.
- Equities backtests run pit_mode=research (lookahead bias, not
  promotion-eligible). A real-money claim needs a PIT run.
- New behaviour ships default-OFF, enabled per-document.


DO NOT RETRY (all measured)

- Loosening the entry-extension gate: blocked basket returned -7.95%.
- Exempting sells or the opening basket from the turnover budget: leaves 7.1%
  charged against a 50% budget, so the brake never binds again.
- Raising max_positions: latches breach auto-heal into per-bar forced
  liquidation and dilutes the prize ~62%.
- Entry gates to beat SPY in a bull: made both test windows worse.
- The asymmetric-objective search (beat B&H in every bull AND positive in every
  bear): ~60 configs across 9 approaches, proven unreachable.

HOW TO VALIDATE

Paired runs on the same window, instance, granularity and cash. Reference:
2026-01-01..2026-03-01, v2-let-run-core, 3600s, $6,000.

Use at least 3 windows, including at least 1 out-of-sample and at least 1 where
leadership is not semiconductors - otherwise you are fitting to one name.

Nexus state is shared and mutable across runs. Give each arm its own
history_scope_salt, and make both arms equally warm or equally cold.

n=5 round trips is not evidence. Do not promote a lever on one window.

Read the run. Config-based predictions have been wrong more often than right on
this codebase. Pull the log and confirm what actually happened before believing
any mechanism.
