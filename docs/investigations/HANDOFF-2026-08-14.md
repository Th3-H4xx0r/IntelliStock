## 1. THE CORE FINDING (D1) — the conversion bug, six independent agents
================================================================================
`_CORE_MIN_SATELLITE_TRIM_USD = 25.0` (broker.py ~3255) lets the satellite cap trim a buy's
`cash_per_trade` down to any room above $25. ~560 lines later `_exec_min_position_floor`
(broker.py ~3732) refuses anything under `max($50, NAV*min_position_nav_pct)` ~ $370. The two
constants are 15x apart and neither knows the other. Result: the cap sizes a conviction buy into a
band ($30-$260) the floor structurally refuses ($361-$383). The core is SOLD to raise cash for these
buys and they die anyway.
Evidence: bt 523085 — 44 of 44 trims that reached the floor refused on the NEXT line, $67,416 blocked
(72% of all refused notional); bt 718107 — 32/68; SNDK refused 9 times, captured +2.28% of a +166%
move. Corpus correction (reviewer B, 92 logs, 534 trims): the skip WAS already reported as
`insufficient_cash`, and 70 of 534 trims DID fill — so "zero ever filled" was false on the wide
corpus, but the majority still die and the mechanism is real.

THE FIX (committed, default-OFF, flag `conversion_fixes_enabled`): at the satellite-cap site, when
the NAV floor is in play (`min_position_nav_pct > 0`) AND room < floor AND the name is not held,
DECLINE the buy and report the skip in the sibling schema, instead of emitting an unfillable order.
Does NOT lower the floor (a 2.8%-of-NAV buy is the "noise" the objective rejects). Two reviewers
verified: no NameError, flag-off byte-identical, skip schema matches the sibling site, exception
defaults fall to old behaviour, log shape matches the live SKIP BUY line so simulate_allocation.py
does not break. Tests: backend/tests/test_satellite_cap_floor.py (10). Commit 10b315f.
