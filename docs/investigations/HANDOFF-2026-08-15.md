```

satellite_design_share = 1 - core_target_pct(0.35) - 0.02    = 0.63
```

## 5. OPEN DEFECT — the fill-boundary cash sign flip (TOP CODE TASK)
================================================================================
`ValueError: confirmed buy fill exceeds available cash` (`portfolio_emulator.py:1169`) killed
bt 101666 at 2026-04-20. It is **not** a guard working correctly:

`pending_sell_proceeds` **CREDITS** an unfilled sale into `get_buying_power`, but once that sale
fills the proceeds become unsettled and `_withheld_cash()` **DEBITS** them. The same dollars flip
sign across the fill boundary, so a buy sized against the credit is unaffordable when it fills — and
`apply_fill` **raises rather than clamps**, killing the entire run.

Latent while the book barely traded; reachable the moment the core actually deploys to target.
Worked around for measurement by setting `backtest_credit_pending_sell_proceeds=false` on both arms.
**That is a workaround, not a fix**, and it is the sixth change riding along with the five in §3.
The real fix is to make the credit and the withholding consistent across that boundary — a
live-money-adjacent cash-accounting path that needs an adversarial sweep, not a 3am patch.
