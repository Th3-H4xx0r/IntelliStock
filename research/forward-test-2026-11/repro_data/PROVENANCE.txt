f13_sleeves_2009.csv.gz: the 11 ex-equity TSMOM sleeves F13_D trades (US bonds 2/10/20y, six FX carry-adjusted
spot series, GSCI, gold), copied from the strategy hunt's data/F13_*.csv (built by hunt/f13/build_sleeves.py),
rows from 2009-01-01 only (the overlay's EWMA vol has a 60-row centre of mass; 2009-2012 gives >900 rows of
warm-up before VAL starts). Used ONLY by repro_check.py, to show that the frozen copy of the F13 engine
reproduces F13_D's logged VAL row. The live strategies never read it.
